Settings & index-zero calibration
Paste this into the top of the file (block AXIS_CALIBRATION) so the values travel with the page instead of living in one browser:
The index pulse fires at one fixed mechanical position of the shaft, once per full turn. Calibration stores how far that position sits from the arm hanging at rest, so from then on the zero is restored automatically — no manual zeroing, and angles stay comparable between sessions.

How to calibrate one arm: let it hang still at its resting position, press Calibrate, then turn the arm slowly through at least one full revolution. The moment the index passes, the offset is written to the device flash and survives reboots and battery changes.

An arm that never completes a full turn during an experiment keeps the last zero it captured; if it has never seen the index since power-up, fall back to the manual Set zero button.
